# Concept of Operations

Mission: protect an approximately 20 m x 20 m perimeter using about 16 distributed sensing nodes without cameras.

Nodes form one virtual electronic fence. Local sensing and event correlation must continue without Internet. Gateways provide durable store-and-forward, management, and upstream integration. CCOC provides situational awareness, incident management, command/control, maintenance visibility, audit, and degraded-state reporting.
